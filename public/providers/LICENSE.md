# Provider icon sources

The SVG marks in this directory are exported from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) using the `simple-icons` npm package. Simple Icons is licensed under CC0-1.0; individual brand marks remain subject to their owners' trademark rules.

Kimi's mark is also available from its official [brand guide](https://www.kimi.com/resources/kimi-brand).

The OpenAI Blossom is sourced from the official [OpenAI design guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/) and is used only to identify OpenAI models in the leaderboard.

Z.ai and StepFun assets are sourced from their official sites:

- [Z.ai logo](https://z-cdn.chatglm.cn/z-ai/static/logo.svg)
- [StepFun favicon](https://www.stepfun.com/step_favicon.svg)

Providers without a mapped mark use the UI's text monogram fallback instead of an invented logo.
